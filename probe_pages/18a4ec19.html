About GQT Kalamazoo 10

820 Maple Hill Dr
Kalamazoo, MI 49009
(269) 345-6434
kalamazoo@gqtmovies.com
GQT Kalamazoo 10 delivers movies, munchies and memories with a focus in value, cleanliness and customer care. Featuring 10 screens with all digital projection, stadium seating and premium high back loungers. Doors open a half hour prior to first showing. As a reminder, Guests aged 16 and under must be accompanied by a parent or guardian (over 21 years old) for movies starting at 6 PM or later. The guardian must remain present for the entire duration of the movie. If the guardian leaves before the movie ends, any unaccompanied guest 16 years and younger will be asked to exit. No refunds will be provided to guests asked to leave due to rule violations or behavioral issues.

Reserved Seating

A reserved seating movie description means you pre-select your exact seat (like in row D, seat 12) when buying your ticket, so you don't have to rush to get a good spot, ensuring your chosen seat is waiting, even if you arrive right at showtime, turning moviegoing into a more planned event with guaranteed comfort and no seat-searching stress.

General Admission

General Admission (GA) in a theater means no assigned seats, offering lower prices and flexibility, with spots (seated or standing) filled on a first-come, first-served basis, often requiring early arrival for good views but allowing freedom to move.
